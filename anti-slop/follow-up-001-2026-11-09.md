# Anti-Slop Follow-up 001 - KhepiLabs
Date: 2026-11-09
Mode: AFTER (session override)
Approved scope: Findings 1-6 from `anti-slop/audit-001-2026-11-09.md`.

## Outcome

### 1. Em dashes in shipped UI and metadata - addressed
- Replaced the em dash in the HTML title and social metadata with a separator that reads cleanly in browser and share previews.
- Rewrote the homepage hero sentence rather than swapping punctuation only.
- Replaced admin table placeholders with explicit labels such as "Not specified" and "Not available".
- Normalized the em dash in stylesheet section comments.

### 2. Missing design direction and Design Read - addressed
- Added `DESIGN.md` with a Design Read, explicit ENERGY / RHYTHM / MOTION dials, palette, typography, layout, iconography, copy, and responsive rules.
- The direction is based on the existing KhepiLabs geometric mark and root colour tokens, then applied to the affected public information pages and footer.

### 3. Unsupported safety and freshness claims - addressed
- Removed "safe and always updated" from the homepage.
- Replaced "Secure Downloads" with publisher/source information wording that is conditional on listing data.
- Updated supporting feature copy to describe only information that may be present in a listing.

### 4. Iconography without a documented rationale - addressed
- Documented the functional rationale for Lucide in `DESIGN.md`.
- Replaced decorative or safety-coded icons in the reviewed public/admin areas with icons that describe the relevant task or content.
- Kept icons secondary to their visible labels.

### 5. Excessive blur and glass effects - addressed in source
- Removed repeated blur declarations from public cards, navigation, search, floating controls, and other surfaces.
- Kept blur only on the public app detail overlay and the admin modal backdrop.
- Changed the admin top bar and floating accents to solid surfaces.

### 6. Template-like footer and repeated public page composition - addressed
- Replaced the four-column footer with brand context, catalogue navigation, support links, and a compact legal row.
- Applied the same information architecture to the homepage and public information pages.
- Reworked public information pages into an editorial hero, restrained brand mark treatment, content-led section layout, and responsive reflow.
- Replaced generic feature-card claims with specific directory functions.
